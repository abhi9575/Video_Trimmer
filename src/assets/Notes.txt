onTimeUpdate => is an event listener that fires continuously as a video or audio element plays.

//onPointers
1. onPointerMove={handlePointerMove}
What it listens for: Fires continuously whenever the cursor or finger moves over the timeline element.

2. onPointerUp={handlePointerUp}
What it listens for: Fires when the user releases the primary mouse button or lifts their finger off the screen.

3. onPointerCancel={handlePointerUp}
What it listens for: Fires when the browser interrupts a touch or drag interaction.

4. onPointerDown is a modern browser event listener that fires the exact instant a user presses a mouse button down, touches a touchscreen, or presses a stylus onto an element.
